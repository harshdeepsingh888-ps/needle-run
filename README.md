# Needle Run

A deep-space arcade game that runs in the browser.

**Play:** https://harshdeepsingh888-ps.github.io/needle-run/

## Modes

- **Warp**: fly a rocket down a warp corridor and slip through the open slice of each spinning gate. The rocket follows your mouse; on touch, drag anywhere; keys: arrows or WASD.
- **Twin**: two mirrored rockets. Spread them apart or pinch them together to fit each laser fence. Move the mouse or finger up and down; keys: ↑ / ↓ or hold Space.

## Features

- Power-ups: Shield (blocks one hit), Slow-mo (half speed for 5 s), Magnet (pulls you toward the gap for 6 s)
- Generated synthwave soundtrack whose tempo follows your speed
- Five unlockable rocket skins in the Hangar
- Daily run: the same seeded course for everyone each day, with a copyable result

The whole game is a single `index.html` (three.js loaded from a CDN). Scores and unlocks are saved in your browser's local storage.
